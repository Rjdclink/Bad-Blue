### Auto-Payout Engine (APE-60)
**Guarantee:** No hour is ever skipped

**Verification Layers:**

- Verifies address integrity (format, length)

### Tier 2: Multi-Path Transaction Delivery
**Paths:** 3 independent delivery routes

3. **Backup:** Different chain with cross-bridge route

**Priority Mode:**

- High-priority queue placement

### Tier 3: Proof-of-Receipt Loop
**Purpose:** Ensure exact amount received

**Process:**
1. **Confirm** incoming transaction in wallet
2. **Reconcile** expected vs. received amounts
3. **Auto-correct** with supplemental payout if mismatch
4. **Track** reconciliation history

**Auto-correction:**
- Detects amount mismatches
- Calculates difference
- Issues supplemental payout automatically
- Links to original transaction
