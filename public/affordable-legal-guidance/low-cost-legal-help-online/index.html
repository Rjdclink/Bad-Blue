What low-cost legal help can do

Online legal-information tools can help clarify what kind of problem you have, identify the jurisdiction, organize dates and documents, explain general legal concepts, and prepare questions for deeper research or an attorney consultation.

What it should not pretend to do

A self-help platform is not a substitute for representation, privileged individualized advice, emergency relief, or an attorney’s professional judgment in a high-stakes matter. Good low-cost help makes those limits clear.

Use the actual documents

When the issue depends on a notice, order, contract, filing, or letter, upload the material rather than relying on a summary from memory. This can make the research and document work more focused.
