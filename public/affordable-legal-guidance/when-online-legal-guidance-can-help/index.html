When Online Legal Guidance Can Help

Online legal guidance is most useful when you need orientation, research assistance, document analysis, or help preparing information. It is less appropriate when the situation requires representation, emergency action, privileged advice, or professional judgment under serious time pressure.

Start LegalWhat

Good uses for online guidance

Common uses include understanding a legal term or process, organizing a timeline, identifying possible legal issues, researching a statute or rule, reviewing a document, preparing questions for counsel, or creating a first draft for review.

Situations that may need counsel quickly

Imminent filing deadlines, criminal charges, emergency custody or protection matters, threatened loss of housing, major financial exposure, complex litigation, or a need for courtroom representation may justify contacting a licensed attorney promptly.

Use online guidance to prepare better

Even when you plan to hire counsel, organizing the facts, documents, dates, and questions beforehand can make the professional consultation more focused.

Treat the tool as a starting point

The strongest use of legal technology is not pretending uncertainty does not exist. It is making the problem easier to understand, surfacing what still needs verification, and helping the user decide what to do next.
