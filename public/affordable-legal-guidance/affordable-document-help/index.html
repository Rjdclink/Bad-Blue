Affordable Legal Document Help

Legal documents often require more than filling blanks into a generic form. LegalWhat can help identify what kind of document is needed, gather the relevant facts, account for jurisdiction, prepare a structured draft, and support review before use.

Start LegalWhat

Identify the document before drafting

If you know the document name, start there. If you do not, describe the situation and what you need the document to accomplish. The first task is determining whether a local form is required or whether a custom draft is appropriate.

Use the governing facts

Names, dates, parties, jurisdiction, requested relief, factual assertions, attachments, service requirements, and deadlines can all affect a document. A useful drafting workflow gathers these details before treating the draft as finished.

Review and revise

A generated document should be read carefully. Check factual assertions, citations, formatting, signature blocks, exhibits, filing requirements, and anything that creates a legal obligation.

Downloadable output

Where the workflow supports it, LegalWhat can prepare finished drafts for PDF or DOCX download so users can review, edit, save, print, or provide the document to counsel.
