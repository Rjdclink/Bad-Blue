Turn a Legal Conversation Into a Document

A legal conversation can be used to collect the facts and instructions that a later document draft needs.

Start a Legal Conversation

Practical checklist

  • Identify the document’s purpose before drafting.
  • Confirm the parties, jurisdiction, dates, and requested outcome.
  • Use the earlier conversation to build the factual section or timeline.
  • Review the draft for facts and jurisdiction before export.
  • Choose editable DOCX or fixed-layout PDF based on what you need to do next.

Why this improves the conversation

Clear facts and source documents reduce ambiguity. They also make it easier to separate legal research from factual uncertainty, identify the correct jurisdiction, and decide whether the next step is another question, document analysis, or document drafting.

Keep the workflow reviewable

Preserve the source documents, confirm important dates, and review generated drafts before using them. Where a court or agency requires an official form, use the current official version and follow its instructions.
