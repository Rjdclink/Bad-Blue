Legal Questions About Deadlines

Deadlines are among the most important details in a legal problem because missing one can affect rights or procedure. LegalWhat can help identify which source controls the timing question and what facts are needed to calculate it.

Ask a Legal Question

Identify what triggered the deadline

A deadline may run from service, filing, entry of an order, receipt of notice, an event date, publication, discovery of harm, or another legally defined trigger.

Use the right rule

Statutes, court rules, local rules, agency regulations, contracts, and orders can all create different deadlines. General internet summaries should not substitute for the controlling source.

Account for service and calendar rules

Weekend, holiday, electronic-service, mail-service, extension, and computation rules can affect the final date in some jurisdictions.

Treat urgent deadlines differently

If a filing date may be imminent and the consequences are serious, verify the rule immediately and consider contacting the clerk, agency, or licensed attorney rather than relying only on general online information.
