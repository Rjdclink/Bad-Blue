Get Legal Answers Before Hiring a Lawyer

Sometimes you already know you may need counsel, but you still want to understand the situation before paying for professional time. LegalWhat can help organize the matter so you know what questions to bring to an attorney.

Ask a Legal Question

Understand the basic legal framework

Learn which area of law is involved, what jurisdiction applies, what general rule or procedure may matter, and what deadlines or documents deserve immediate attention.

Separate research questions from strategy

General legal research can explain rules and procedures. Strategic decisions about litigation, settlement, privilege, negotiation, or professional responsibility may require individualized attorney advice.

Prepare your documents and timeline

A concise timeline and organized source documents can make a consultation more efficient and reduce time spent reconstructing basic facts.

Know what still needs professional judgment

A useful preparation process should make uncertainty visible rather than create false confidence. Write down the questions that remain unresolved and require counsel.
