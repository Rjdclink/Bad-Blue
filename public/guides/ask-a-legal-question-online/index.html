Ask a Legal Question Online

You do not need to translate your problem into perfect legal terminology before asking for help. Start with what happened, where it happened, when it happened, and what you are trying to understand.

Ask a Legal Question

Give the facts that change the answer

Jurisdiction, dates, existing court or agency action, documents, notices, payments, prior agreements, and deadlines can all change which law or procedure matters.

Ask one concrete question first

A broad story is useful context, but a focused question makes the research more efficient. Examples include what deadline applies, what a notice means, what elements must be shown, or what document may be needed.

Use follow-up questions to narrow the issue

If a key fact is missing, continue the conversation rather than restarting. Clarifying the jurisdiction or procedural posture can turn a vague question into a useful research task.

Bring in documents when wording matters

If the question depends on a contract, order, notice, filing, letter, or other document, use the actual file so exact language can be reviewed.
