Finding correctional records

The inmate-locator service is intended to help users locate correctional information across federal, state, local, and participating sources. Useful search details can include a full name, approximate age, jurisdiction, facility, or known identifying number.

Custody status and release information can change quickly. Confirm important information with the responsible correctional agency or official record source before relying on it.
