What a background-report workflow is for

Pantheon is designed to organize public-source research into a consolidated report so users can review information in one place rather than treating scattered search results as a finished conclusion.

Public records can be incomplete, outdated, or associated with people who have similar names. Verify important findings against authoritative records and use background information only for lawful purposes.
