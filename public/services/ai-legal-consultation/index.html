Talk to Lexara: a visible, conversational legal AI

Conversational legal information across LegalWhat's 40 legal areas, with text and voice interaction.

Lexara is designed for people who may not know the technical name of the tool they need. You can speak or type naturally about a legal problem and receive responsive legal information across 40 areas of law while interacting with a visible, animated AI presentation whose face and mouth move during spoken responses.

Ask legal questions in ordinary language

Use Lexara when you want an AI you can actually talk to about a legal question, case, procedure, filing, evidence, contract, court paper, or other legal issue. The conversation can continue naturally by voice or text rather than requiring a series of isolated search queries.

Create legal documents from the conversation

When the conversation indicates that a document is needed, Lexara can help prepare and format the legal document. Finished documents can be made downloadable in DOCX or PDF format for review, editing, saving, or use in the appropriate workflow.

Upload documents, evidence, images, and other media

You can upload your own material for analysis and review. Lexara can explain what the material contains, identify issues that deserve attention, help revise or optimize supported documents and media, and make resulting files available for download. This supports searches such as “AI that reads my legal document,” “AI that reviews my evidence,” “AI that fixes a legal document,” and “AI that analyzes court papers.”
