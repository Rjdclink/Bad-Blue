Talk Through a Legal Problem Online

You do not need to know the legal name of your problem before you begin. Describe what happened in ordinary language, then use the conversation to organize the facts and narrow the questions that actually need research.

Start a Legal Conversation

Start with what happened

Explain the event in the order that makes sense to you. Names, dates, documents, prior court action, agency involvement, and the result you want are often more useful at the beginning than legal terminology.

Turn a story into legal issues

A conversational workflow can separate factual questions from legal questions and identify which details may affect jurisdiction, procedure, deadlines, defenses, remedies, or document needs.

Keep the context while you refine the question

Instead of rewriting the entire problem for every new query, continue the discussion as new facts become relevant. This is especially useful when a matter has a timeline, multiple documents, or several related legal issues.

Know when to escalate

Some matters require a licensed attorney, emergency court action, privileged advice, or representation. A useful online conversation should help you recognize those situations rather than pretending every problem can be resolved by a self-help tool.
