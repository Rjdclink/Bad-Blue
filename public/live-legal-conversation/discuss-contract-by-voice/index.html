Discuss a Contract by Voice

LegalWhat is designed to talk through contract language and concerns using voice rather than typing every question. The conversation can continue as new facts, documents, and jurisdiction details are added instead of forcing the user to restart with every question.

Start Talking With Lexara

How the conversation can work

  1. identify the contract and business context
  2. upload the agreement when available
  3. ask about specific clauses or risks
  4. continue with proposed revisions or a replacement draft

Example workflow

A user can ask about a termination clause, clarify what happened during performance, and then request revised language for review.

Move between conversation and documents

If the answer depends on exact language, upload the relevant supported document. If the conversation shows that a new document is needed, continue into drafting and request an editable DOCX or a fixed-layout PDF where supported.

Correct the record as you go

Names, dates, courts, states, and procedural posture can materially change the answer. Correct them as soon as they change so later research and drafting use the updated context.
