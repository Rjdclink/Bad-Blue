Voice and Text Legal Help Online

Different parts of a legal problem call for different ways of communicating. Voice is useful for explaining the story; text is useful for exact names, dates, citations, and document language. LegalWhat supports both within the same legal-information workflow.

Start a Legal Conversation

Use voice for context

Speak naturally when you need to explain a long factual background, a sequence of events, or what you are trying to accomplish.

Use text for precision

Type exact statute numbers, case names, addresses, quoted clauses, docket information, or other details that should not depend on speech recognition.

Keep one matter coherent

The value comes from keeping the legal question, supporting facts, research, files, and document work connected instead of fragmenting the matter across unrelated tools.

Review before acting

Whether information was provided by voice or text, important legal conclusions should be checked against current authority, court rules, filing requirements, and the actual documents involved.
