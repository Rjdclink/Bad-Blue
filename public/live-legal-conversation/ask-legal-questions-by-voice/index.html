Ask Legal Questions by Voice

Speaking can be easier than typing when a legal problem involves a long timeline or many details. LegalWhat supports voice conversation so you can explain the situation naturally and continue with follow-up questions.

Start a Legal Conversation

When voice is useful

Voice can make it easier to describe what happened, especially when you are working from memory or trying to explain a sequence of events. You can still provide exact dates, names, or quotations when precision matters.

A conversation, not dictation

The point of voice interaction is not merely converting speech to text. The conversation can respond to what you said, ask for missing information, and keep the issue moving toward a clearer legal research question.

Pair voice with documents

If the answer depends on a notice, contract, order, pleading, or other file, use the actual document rather than trying to read every sentence aloud. File analysis can preserve exact language while voice handles the broader explanation.

Privacy and accuracy

Avoid sharing information you do not need to provide. For important facts, verify names, dates, amounts, and quoted language before relying on a response or using a generated document.
