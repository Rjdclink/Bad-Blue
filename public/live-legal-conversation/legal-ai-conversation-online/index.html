Legal AI Conversation Online

LegalWhat is designed to have a continuing legal conversation instead of entering isolated one-off prompts. The conversation can continue as new facts, documents, and jurisdiction details are added instead of forcing the user to restart with every question.

Start Talking With Lexara

How the conversation can work

  1. describe the problem in ordinary language
  2. answer short follow-up questions that clarify the facts
  3. change or add details as the conversation develops
  4. move from questions into research, file analysis, or document drafting

Example workflow

A user can begin with a broad civil dispute, then clarify the state, upload the complaint, ask what the document means, and continue into a response or document workflow.

Move between conversation and documents

If the answer depends on exact language, upload the relevant supported document. If the conversation shows that a new document is needed, continue into drafting and request an editable DOCX or a fixed-layout PDF where supported.

Correct the record as you go

Names, dates, courts, states, and procedural posture can materially change the answer. Correct them as soon as they change so later research and drafting use the updated context.
