Talk Through an Appeal

LegalWhat is designed to organize appellate questions around the order, deadline, preserved issues, and record. The conversation can continue as new facts, documents, and jurisdiction details are added instead of forcing the user to restart with every question.

Start Talking With Lexara

How the conversation can work

  1. identify the judgment or order
  2. confirm entry and notice dates
  3. identify objections or issues raised below
  4. separate appealability, timing, and merits questions

Example workflow

A user can discuss a final order, upload it, identify the date entered, and then ask which issues were preserved for review.

Move between conversation and documents

If the answer depends on exact language, upload the relevant supported document. If the conversation shows that a new document is needed, continue into drafting and request an editable DOCX or a fixed-layout PDF where supported.

Correct the record as you go

Names, dates, courts, states, and procedural posture can materially change the answer. Correct them as soon as they change so later research and drafting use the updated context.
