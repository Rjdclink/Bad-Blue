Use LegalWhat to work through immigration law questions in a continuing voice or text conversation. Start with what happened, add the jurisdiction and important dates, and upload the controlling document when the answer depends on exact wording.

These are examples of issue categories, not predetermined answers. The useful starting point is the user’s actual facts and the specific question they need to understand.

What to have ready

  • immigration notices and receipt numbers

Why jurisdiction matters

Immigration matters are primarily federal but depend on the exact benefit, status, agency, court, and procedural history.

When the state, court, agency, or location changes during the conversation, the research and document workflow should change with it rather than continuing under the earlier jurisdiction.

  • response outline
  • document summary
