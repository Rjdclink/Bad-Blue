Professional License Search and Verification

Professional licenses are often public records maintained by state boards or agencies. LegalWhat can help locate the responsible licensing authority and organize publicly available status, credential, and disciplinary information.

Start a Research Conversation

Use public-record research responsibly. LegalWhat is designed to organize and explain lawful public-source information. Availability, completeness, identity matching, and record status can vary by source and jurisdiction.

Use the correct licensing board

Nurses, physicians, attorneys, contractors, real-estate professionals, teachers, and other licensed occupations may be regulated by different boards. Searching the right authority is more reliable than relying on a generic people-search database.

Check status and dates

A license may be active, inactive, expired, suspended, revoked, restricted, or otherwise subject to conditions. Effective dates and renewal periods matter when describing current status.

Discipline is separate from license existence

A person can hold a valid license and still have public disciplinary history, or may have no discipline at all. Verify each issue separately rather than inferring one from the other.

Confirm identity

Where the board provides location, license number, specialty, employer, or credential details, use them to reduce the risk of matching the wrong person.
