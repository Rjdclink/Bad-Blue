Statute, Regulation and Court Rule Research

Statutes and rules often answer the core questions in a legal matter: what must be proven, what deadline applies, which court has authority, what notice is required, and what procedure must be followed. LegalWhat can help organize that research around the correct jurisdiction.

Start Legal Research

Identify the governing source

Federal statutes, state codes, regulations, court rules, local rules, agency regulations, and municipal ordinances may all apply to different parts of a problem.

Read definitions and cross-references

A rule may depend on definitions or other sections that are easy to miss when only one provision is quoted. Cross-references, exceptions, and incorporated procedures should be checked.

Confirm effective dates

A current code section may differ from the version that applied when an event occurred. Effective dates and amendments can matter in disputes involving older conduct.

Turn the rule into a checklist

When a statute or rule contains elements, deadlines, notice requirements, or procedural steps, converting them into a fact-specific checklist can make the research easier to apply.
