Create a Legal Document Online

Creating a useful legal document starts with the problem the document is supposed to solve. LegalWhat can help turn a plain-language description into a structured drafting workflow instead of requiring you to start from a blank page.

Start a Document Conversation

Explain the purpose

State what you need the document to accomplish: demand payment, respond to a filing, request records, memorialize an agreement, ask a court for relief, provide sworn facts, or document another legal action.

Answer focused drafting questions

A structured drafting workflow should ask only for facts that affect the document: parties, jurisdiction, dates, terms, allegations, requested relief, attachments, service method, and any existing case or agency information.

Bring in source documents

Existing orders, contracts, notices, correspondence, forms, or prior filings can provide the exact names, dates, language, and procedural context needed for a more accurate draft.

Review before signing or filing

Check every factual assertion and legal reference. Confirm that the correct form is being used, that local filing instructions are satisfied, and that any required signature, notarization, exhibit, fee, or service step is complete.
