Legal Document Generator

LegalWhat can move from a legal conversation into document preparation when the facts call for a letter, motion, complaint, affidavit, contract, petition, memorandum, request, or another legal document. The workflow is designed to gather the facts first and then prepare a reviewable draft.

Start a Document Conversation

Describe the situation, not just the document name

If you know exactly what document you need, say so. If you do not, explain what happened and what you want the document to accomplish. A useful generator should identify whether a maintained jurisdiction-specific form is required before drafting a generic substitute.

Gather the facts that belong in the document

Names, dates, addresses, procedural history, requested relief, payment terms, factual allegations, attachments, governing law, and signature requirements may all matter. The quality of a legal draft depends heavily on the accuracy of these inputs.

Use jurisdiction-aware drafting

Court rules, agency requirements, statutory language, local forms, caption formats, service rules, and filing procedures can vary. LegalWhat is designed to account for jurisdiction rather than treating every legal document as interchangeable.

Review, revise, and download

A draft is not finished merely because it was generated. Review facts, citations, formatting, clauses, attachments, signatures, and deadlines. Where supported, LegalWhat can provide PDF or DOCX output for further review and editing.
