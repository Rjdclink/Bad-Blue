LegalWhat Capabilities

LegalWhat brings several legal-information workflows into one place: live two-way conversation, affordable legal guidance, legal document generation, public-record and background research, and analysis of legal files and evidence. Each capability has its own public resource pages so you can understand what the platform does before starting a private conversation.

Start a Legal Conversation

Live two-way legal conversation

Talk by voice or text instead of reducing a legal problem to a single search query. Lexara can ask follow-up questions, keep track of the facts you provide, and help organize the issue around the jurisdiction, dates, documents, and outcome you are trying to understand.

  • Voice or text interaction
  • Follow-up questions when facts are missing
  • Jurisdiction-aware issue organization
  • Conversation that can lead into research or document work

Affordable legal guidance and answers

LegalWhat is designed as a lower-cost starting point for understanding legal questions. The current offer is a 3-day free trial, then $19.99/month for full access. The goal is to make useful legal information, research, and document help available without beginning every question with an hourly attorney consultation.

Legal document generation

When the facts call for a document, LegalWhat can help identify the type of document, gather needed details, consider jurisdiction-specific requirements, prepare a draft, and make supported output available as PDF or DOCX for review and editing.

Background and public-record research

LegalWhat can help research publicly available information relevant to a legal question, including court records, incarceration information, professional licenses, business entities, and other public-source facts. Results should be verified against the underlying official source when accuracy is important.

Legal research, files, and evidence

Upload supported files when the question depends on a court paper, contract, notice, image, evidence item, or other document. LegalWhat can help summarize, organize, identify legal issues, and connect the material to further jurisdiction-aware research.

How the capabilities connect in one matter

  1. Conversation: explain the problem by voice or text and establish the jurisdiction, timeline, people, documents, and immediate goal.
  2. File analysis: add the actual order, contract, notice, pleading, image, or other supported material when exact wording or evidence matters.
  3. Research: identify the legal issue and check relevant statutes, rules, cases, agency material, court sources, or public records.
  4. Document work: when the facts call for a response, motion, letter, agreement, memorandum, or other document, carry the matter context into drafting and revision.
  5. Output and review: use PDF or DOCX where supported, then verify the final draft against current jurisdiction-specific requirements before relying on it.

What should remain visible to the user

The useful output is the legal information, factual result, document analysis, or draft that answers the user’s question. Internal research mechanics, confidence calculations, provider routing, and system implementation details are not a substitute for a clear answer and ordinarily do not need to be shown unless they are necessary to explain an uncertainty or limitation.

Reliability depends on the source material

LegalWhat is most useful when the conversation includes the correct jurisdiction, exact dates, current procedural stage, and the actual documents that control the issue. Important legal propositions should be checked against current primary or official sources, and generated documents should be reviewed against current court or agency requirements before use.

When the platform is a starting point rather than the endpoint

LegalWhat can help users become informed, organized, and prepared. It does not appear in court, create an attorney-client relationship, or replace a licensed attorney where representation, emergency relief, privileged individualized advice, or professional judgment is required.
